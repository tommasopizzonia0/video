# The image: motion design, art direction, typography, colour

The map of what a complete motion designer, art director and colorist knows. Apply what fits the brief;
cite the principle when you propose.

## 1. Composition
- Rule of thirds, centre composition for symmetry and for vertical social video, golden ratio; leading lines,
  framing, depth (foreground/midground/background), negative space; visual weight and balance.
- Gestalt: proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, figure/ground; the eye goes to contrast, faces,
  text, movement, warm/bright areas first.
- One focal point per frame; hierarchy by size, contrast, position, motion. Headroom and lead room.
- Vertical 9:16: subject in the central band; platform UI covers top ~14% and bottom ~20-35%, right column
  on TikTok; 4:5 feed crops; design for the smallest safe area when one video feeds many formats.

## 2. Typography
- Classification: serif (trust, premium, editorial), sans (modern, clean), slab, display, script (rarely in
  video), mono (tech). Max two families, use weights for hierarchy.
- Size on phone 1080×1920: body ≥ 48 px, subtitles 50-70 px, titles 80-160 px; x-height and weight matter
  more than point size. Line length 3-8 words, line height 1.05-1.2 for titles, tracking slightly open for
  caps. Kerning on big titles. Avoid widows/orphans, break lines by meaning.
- Legibility on video: contrast (WCAG 4.5:1 small, 3:1 large), stroke/outline, drop shadow, backing plate,
  darkened image behind text; never thin weights on busy footage.
- Captions: burned-in for social (most watch muted), 1-2 lines, word highlighting for retention, consistent
  position above the UI.

## 3. Colour theory and grading
- Hue, saturation, value; colour wheels; harmonies: complementary (teal/orange), analogous, triadic,
  split-complementary; 60-30-10 rule; warm advances, cool recedes; colour psychology per brand and culture.
- Grading workflow: technical correction first (exposure, white balance, contrast, matching shots), then
  the look (creative grade), then secondaries (skin, sky, foliage), then finishing (vignette, grain).
- Scopes: waveform (exposure), vectorscope (hue/saturation, skin tone line), histogram, parade (balance).
- Skin tones on the skin line; greens natural (foliage tends to cyan on cheap cameras); avoid clipped
  highlights and crushed blacks on phone screens; Rec.709 for web, gamma 2.4/sRGB differences.
- LUTs as starting points, not finished looks; consistent look across the whole video.

## 4. Motion design
- The 12 principles of animation (Disney): squash and stretch, anticipation, staging, straight ahead vs
  pose to pose, follow-through and overlapping action, slow in/slow out, arcs, secondary action, timing,
  exaggeration, solid drawing, appeal.
- Easing: ease-out for entrances (fast then settle), ease-in for exits, ease-in-out for moves; overshoot
  for playful brands, linear only for mechanical/constant motion.
- Timing: UI-like elements 200-400 ms, headlines 400-800 ms, staggering 30-80 ms per word/letter; exits
  faster than entrances; hold time for reading.
- Consistency: one motion language (same curves, directions, durations) across the video; motion should
  guide the eye to the next information; nothing moves without a reason.
- Kinetic typography, masks/reveals, parallax, camera moves in 2.5D, transitions built from shapes.
- Motion blur on fast moves for realism; frame rate and shutter (180° rule: shutter = 1/(2×fps)).

## 5. Brand and art direction
- Brand system: logo usage and clear space, colours (primary/accent), fonts, tone of imagery, iconography.
- Product hero shots: clean background, rim light, the product large, benefit text next to it.
- Social ad visual patterns: big bold text, faces, before/after, UGC look vs polished studio, price and
  offer badges, trust signals (reviews, shipping, guarantee).
- Accessibility: contrast, no flashing over 3 times per second (epilepsy guidelines), captions.

## 6. Genre and platform practice
- Ads: product and benefit clear in any frame, CTA designed as a button-like element, brand consistent.
- Reels/TikTok: native look, captions, punchy zoom-ins, readable at a glance.
- YouTube: lower thirds, chapters/titles, thumbnail frame with face + 3-4 words.
- Documentary: restrained titles and lower thirds, natural grade.
- Luxury: space, slow motion, thin serif, desaturated warm grade. Tech: grid, mono, precise motion, cool
  palette. Food/nature: warm, saturated but natural, macro detail.
