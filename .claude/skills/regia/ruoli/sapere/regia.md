# Directing: the whole field, as a checklist of knowledge

The map of what a complete director knows, for any kind of video. Use it to set the intention and to judge
every proposal.

## 1. Intention and audience
- Every video answers: who is it for, what should they feel, what should they remember, what should they
  do. One message per video; everything that does not serve it goes.
- Audience insight: their problem, desire and objection; speak their language; show, don't tell.
- Persuasion: Cialdini (reciprocity, commitment, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity, unity);
  benefit over feature; specificity builds trust (numbers, places, names); emotion first, reason to
  justify. Ethical: no false claims or invented urgency.

## 2. Story
- Story structures: three acts, hero's journey (customer is the hero, brand the guide), problem-solution,
  before-after, a day in the life, a list/countdown, a demonstration, a testimonial, a reveal.
- Conflict and tension create attention; curiosity gaps; setup and payoff; the rule of three.
- Tone: humour, warmth, awe, urgency, calm authority: choose one and keep it.

## 3. Genres
- Advertising (performance vs brand), social organic, product demo, explainer/tutorial, documentary and
  brand film, interview, testimonial, event recap/aftermovie, trailer/teaser, music video, corporate,
  real estate, food, fashion, travel. Each has its own pace, look and sound (see the other role files).
- Platform native behaviour: TikTok/Reels (fast, native, sound-on culture, captions), YouTube (watch time,
  retention curve, chapters), Facebook/Instagram feed (muted autoplay, 4:5), TV (legal loudness, 16:9),
  website hero videos (loop, no sound), cinema.

## 4. Visual and sound language
- Shot choice tells the story: wide = context, close = emotion, insert = detail, POV = immersion; camera
  movement has meaning (push in = importance, pull out = reveal/loneliness, handheld = urgency/realism,
  static = calm/authority).
- Light and colour set mood (high-key/low-key, warm/cool); sound carries half of the emotion.
- Consistency: one style throughout, deliberate breaks only.

## 5. Working with the team
- Give each department the brief and the reason, not only the instruction.
- Decide by priority: emotion > story > rhythm > technique; brand and platform constraints are hard limits.
- Review with fresh eyes: watch muted, watch with sound on a phone, watch at 1× without stopping; ask
  "what did I remember?" and "would I stop scrolling?".
- Fewer, stronger changes; protect what already works.
